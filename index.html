常見問題 / FAQ

通知為什麼會延遲？ iOS 決定背景喚醒的時機，通常是數十分鐘到數小時；強制關閉 App 後就不會再收到通知。
Why are notifications delayed? iOS decides when to wake the app in the background (often tens of minutes to hours), and never after you force-quit it.

怎麼新增或登出帳號？ 點下方帳號按鈕 → 管理帳號。
Add or remove an account: tap the account button → Manage accounts.
